# Business Readiness Evaluation

Professional check-mark evaluation requirements for public, private, and enterprise business launches, covering security, privacy, operations, governance, and release readiness.

This repository provides a structured evaluation framework for assessing whether a business, product, or launch initiative is ready to operate in a production environment. It is designed to help teams review readiness across high-priority operational and governance areas before public, private, or enterprise launch.

## Purpose

The evaluation framework helps teams identify gaps in:

- Security readiness
- Privacy and compliance readiness
- Operations and support readiness
- Governance and accountability
- Release and launch readiness

It is intended to support planning, auditing, and internal review before launch or expansion.

## Evaluation Areas

### 1. Security Readiness

Assess whether the business has the controls required to protect customers, data, and systems.

Checklist items may include:
- Authentication and authorization controls
- Access management policies
- Vulnerability management
- Secure deployment practices
- Incident response planning
- Logging and monitoring

### 2. Privacy Readiness

Review whether personal and sensitive data is handled responsibly and in compliance with applicable standards.

Checklist items may include:
- Data classification and handling policies
- Consent and notice requirements
- Retention and deletion processes
- Data minimization practices
- Third-party data sharing controls
- Privacy review and governance

### 3. Operations Readiness

Confirm that the business can reliably operate the service or launch.

Checklist items may include:
- Service ownership and responsibilities
- Monitoring and alerting
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Runbooks and escalation paths
- Support model and response expectations
- Capacity planning and resilience

### 4. Governance Readiness

Check whether the necessary oversight and accountability mechanisms are in place.

Checklist items may include:
- Executive sponsorship
- Risk ownership
- Policy documentation
- Decision rights and approval flows
- Regulatory and legal review
- Change management controls

### 5. Release Readiness

Verify that the launch is operationally and strategically ready to proceed.

Checklist items may include:
- Launch criteria completion
- Customer-facing readiness
- Communications and rollout plan
- Quality and acceptance checks
- Launch contingency planning
- Post-launch monitoring plan

## Recommended Use

Use this repository as:

- A launch checklist
- A review template for internal teams
- A governance and audit aid
- A readiness scorecard before release
- A shared framework for cross-functional evaluation

## Suggested Review Model

Each domain can be evaluated with a simple status model:

- Pass
- Needs attention
- Risk identified
- Not applicable

A final scorecard can be used to summarize the overall launch readiness.

## Getting Started

1. Review each readiness category.
2. Assign status for each checklist item.
3. Document evidence, owners, and due dates.
4. Prioritize gaps by risk and impact.
5. Approve only when critical gaps are closed.
